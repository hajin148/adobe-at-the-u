What's an Adobe Student Ambassador?

A student who partners with Adobe to bring creative skills to campus. I host workshops, run challenges, and help classmates get comfortable with tools like Photoshop, Premiere Pro, Illustrator and Adobe Express.

Who is this for?

Everyone. Whether you're building a class presentation, a club flyer, a résumé, a portfolio or your first short video — you don't need any design background to join.
